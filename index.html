CARDS AGAINST
SOCIETY

The party game where chaos wins.

How to play

1

Pick a judge

One player becomes the judge for the round and reads the black card aloud.

2

Choose your funniest white card

Everyone else picks the white card that best fits the prompt.

3

Vote and score

The judge picks the winner, the room cheers, and the round resets with fresh cards.

Example cards

Black card

What’s the most embarrassing thing to find in your fridge?

White card

A tiny horse with a huge attitude.

White card

A surprise tax audit.
